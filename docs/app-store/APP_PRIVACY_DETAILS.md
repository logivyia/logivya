# App Store App Privacy Details

This document prepares App Store privacy questionnaire answers. Final answers must be approved by the legal/product owner before submission.

## Name

- Purpose: account profile, support, authentication context.
- Linked to user: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: only service providers required to operate the app.
- Retention: retained while account is active and as required by legal/business obligations.

## Email Address

- Purpose: login, password reset, account communication, support.
- Linked to user: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: email delivery providers if used.
- Retention: retained while account is active and as required for security/audit.

## Phone Number

- Purpose: profile, account communication, WhatsApp pairing workflow support.
- Linked to user: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: only where required to provide the requested service.
- Retention: retained while account is active or until deletion request is completed.

## Company Information

- Purpose: workspace management, billing readiness, subscription support.
- Linked to user/company: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: payment, invoice, hosting, or compliance providers where applicable.
- Retention: subject to account lifecycle and legal retention requirements.

## User-Entered Messages

- Purpose: message creation, scheduling, delivery history, operational reporting.
- Linked to user/company: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: only messaging infrastructure needed to execute user-requested actions.
- Retention: retained according to workspace history, audit, and deletion settings.

## Support Messages

- Purpose: customer support and troubleshooting.
- Linked to user: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: support/hosting providers if used.
- Retention: retained for support history and compliance.

## Subscription and Payment Metadata

- Purpose: subscription status, billing support, invoice readiness, access control.
- Linked to user/company: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: payment and billing providers where applicable.
- Retention: retained according to financial/legal obligations.

## Device Identifiers

- Purpose: secure mobile sessions, notification delivery, diagnostics, abuse prevention.
- Linked to user: may be linked to user account/session.
- Tracking: no, unless future analytics configuration changes.
- Shared with third parties: push, analytics, or crash reporting providers if enabled.
- Retention: retained while device session is active or until logout/unregister.

## Push Notification Tokens

- Purpose: push notification delivery.
- Linked to user/device: yes.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: push notification provider.
- Retention: removed on logout/device unregister when backend cleanup succeeds.

## Crash Diagnostics

- Purpose: crash monitoring, reliability, debugging.
- Linked to user: should be minimized; may include user/session context if configured.
- Tracking: no.
- Shared with third parties: crash reporting provider if enabled.
- Retention: according to crash reporting provider settings.

## Analytics Events

- Purpose: app performance, feature usage, release monitoring.
- Linked to user: should be minimized and pseudonymized where possible.
- Tracking: no third-party advertising tracking.
- Shared with third parties: analytics provider if enabled.
- Retention: according to analytics provider settings.
