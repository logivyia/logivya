# App Review Risk Analysis

## Risk: WhatsApp Automation Perception

Apple may question whether Logivya automates messaging in a way that violates third-party platform policies.

Mitigation:

- Present Logivya as a business communication management platform.
- Explain that users connect their own authorized accounts.
- Explain that users control messages, groups, recipients, and scheduling.
- Do not claim official WhatsApp or Meta partnership.

## Risk: Third-Party Platform Connection

The app references WhatsApp account management and connection flows.

Mitigation:

- Add disclaimer in metadata: Logivya is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta, Facebook, Instagram or Telegram.
- Avoid third-party logos in app icon, screenshots, feature graphics, or app title.
- Keep copy factual and user-controlled.

## Risk: Messaging Features

Messaging and scheduling features can be reviewed for spam or unsolicited messaging concerns.

Mitigation:

- Describe messaging as user-controlled business communication.
- Include account controls, support, and responsible use terms.
- Do not market spam, scraping, or unauthorized outreach.

## Risk: User-Generated Content

Users enter message content and support messages.

Mitigation:

- Ensure Terms of Service cover acceptable use.
- Provide support and abuse/contact path.
- Avoid showing real customer content in screenshots.

## Risk: Payment and Subscription Status

The app displays subscription status but does not implement in-app purchase in this phase.

Mitigation:

- If digital subscriptions are sold outside the app, verify App Store policy implications before release.
- Avoid purchase buttons that route around Apple's rules unless legally/policy-approved.
- Keep the mobile app as account management and status display until payment policy is finalized.

## Risk: Privacy Disclosures

The app uses account, company, notification, analytics, and crash diagnostics data.

Mitigation:

- Complete App Privacy accurately.
- Publish Privacy Policy.
- Do not submit until legal text is approved.
