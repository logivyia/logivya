# Privacy Policy, Terms and KVKK Requirements

## Required Public URLs

Before Google Play submission, these URLs must be published and reachable:

- Privacy Policy: `https://www.logivya.com/privacy-policy`
- Terms of Service: `https://www.logivya.com/terms-of-service`
- KVKK Disclosure: `https://www.logivya.com/kvkk`

## Current Codebase Status

The web app contains route files for:

- `/privacy-policy`
- `/terms-of-service`
- `/kvkk`

## Required Legal Review

Do not submit until the published legal pages are reviewed for:

- Mobile app usage
- Push notification processing
- Device identifier processing
- WhatsApp connection metadata
- Support ticket data
- Subscription and payment metadata
- Account deletion and data deletion request process

## Google Play Notes

- Add the Privacy Policy URL in Play Console.
- Ensure the URL is accessible without login.
- Do not use draft, private, or localhost URLs.
- Do not fabricate legal compliance; final text must be approved by the business/legal owner.
