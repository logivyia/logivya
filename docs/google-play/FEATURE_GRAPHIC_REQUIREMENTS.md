# Google Play Feature Graphic Requirements

## Required Asset

- Size: 1024 x 500 px
- Format: PNG or JPG
- No transparency
- Safe margins for text and logo

## Suggested Copy

## Turkish

Logivya

Tüm iletişim kanallarınızı ve iş süreçlerinizi tek platformdan yönetin.

## English

Logivya

Manage communication channels and business workflows from one platform.

## Design Requirements

- Use Logivya branding and colors.
- Do not include WhatsApp, Meta, Facebook, Instagram or Telegram logos.
- Do not imply official affiliation with third-party messaging platforms.
- Keep text readable on mobile and desktop Play Store surfaces.
