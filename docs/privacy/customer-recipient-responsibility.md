# Customer Recipient Data Responsibilities

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

The following controls are a draft product and contract framework.

Customers should be required to:

- use contacts, groups and message content only for documented lawful purposes;
- provide required notices and obtain any required commercial-message or marketing permission;
- avoid purchased, scraped or unlawfully shared recipient lists;
- honor opt-outs, objections, deletion requests and suppression lists;
- restrict seats and connected accounts to authorized personnel;
- avoid special-category, confidential or illegal content unless separately approved and protected;
- investigate complaints and provide evidence when reasonably required;
- comply with WhatsApp terms and applicable anti-spam rules.

LOGIVYA engineering controls include tenant isolation, authenticated actions, rate limits, audit trails, purpose-specific optional preferences and an admin privacy workflow. These controls do not replace the customer's legal obligations.

Product decisions still required:

- recipient notice/attestation placement;
- evidence retention and suppression-list design;
- complaint thresholds and automated account restrictions;
- high-risk industry restrictions;
- acceptable-use enforcement and appeal process.

Qualified counsel must approve this allocation before it becomes contractual or user-facing text.
