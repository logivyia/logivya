# Layered Privacy Notice Architecture

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

Public legal wording remains unchanged until counsel approval. Draft architecture:

1. General platform notice: controller identity/contact, account/security/billing/support processing, rights and complaints.
2. Registration just-in-time layer: mandatory data, purpose and link to full notice; separate contract acceptance.
3. WhatsApp connection layer: session credentials, contacts/groups, customer instructions, retention and recipient responsibilities.
4. Messaging layer: targeting/content responsibilities, recipient rights and prohibited use.
5. Support layer: ticket text/attachments and retention.
6. Cookie/browser layer: necessary versus optional controls.
7. Mobile layer: notifications, optional analytics/diagnostics and permissions.
8. Payment layer: provider and invoice/tax handling.
9. Transfer/subprocessor layer: approved provider list and change process.

Each approved document needs a version, effective date, locale, review status, approver and superseded version. Translation is not approval.
