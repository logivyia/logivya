# Terms of Use Architecture Draft

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

This file is not public terms and must not be presented as accepted legal wording.

Counsel should prepare reviewed terms covering at minimum:

- contracting entity, eligibility and authority to bind a company;
- account, seat and administrator responsibilities;
- service scope, availability and supported WhatsApp behavior;
- subscription, renewal, billing, tax, suspension and termination;
- customer content and recipient-data responsibilities;
- acceptable use, anti-spam, complaints and enforcement;
- confidentiality, security and incident cooperation;
- controller/processor allocation and DPA incorporation;
- intellectual property and feedback;
- warranties, liability and indemnity appropriate to each market;
- deletion, export, retention and post-termination handling;
- governing law, disputes, notices and material-change process;
- document version, effective date and language precedence.

Registration must distinguish notice delivery, mandatory contract acceptance and optional consent. Optional analytics or marketing must never be bundled into acceptance of these terms.
