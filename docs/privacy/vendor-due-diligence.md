# Vendor and Processor Due Diligence

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

Complete this checklist for every provider before approval:

- legal entity, service and contract owner;
- controller/processor role and documented instructions;
- processing and backup regions;
- subprocessor list and change-notice process;
- DPA and international-transfer mechanism;
- encryption, tenant isolation, access controls and key ownership;
- retention, deletion, return and export support;
- breach notification timing and contact channel;
- audit reports/certifications and material limitations;
- availability, backup, restore and exit plan;
- data-subject request assistance;
- government-access/transparency assessment;
- approval, expiry and next review date.

Current provider names are inventoried in `subprocessor-register.md`; no provider is marked legally approved by this repository.
