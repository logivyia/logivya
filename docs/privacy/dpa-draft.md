# Data Processing Agreement Framework

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED - DO NOT PUBLISH`

This is a clause checklist, not a contract.

- Parties, services, controller/processor roles and documented customer instructions.
- Subject matter, duration, nature/purpose, data and subject categories.
- Confidentiality, access control, encryption, tenant isolation and security measures.
- Subprocessor authorization/change notice and current register.
- International transfers and approved mechanisms.
- Assistance with data-subject requests, DPIAs and regulator consultation.
- Personal-data breach notice and cooperation.
- Return/deletion at termination, legal exceptions, backups and re-deletion after restore.
- Audit/evidence rights, reasonable limits and confidentiality.
- Customer responsibilities for recipients, message content, marketing rules and lawful instructions.
- Liability, governing law, precedence and termination.

Qualified counsel must draft and approve the final agreement.
