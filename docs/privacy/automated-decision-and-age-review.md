# Automated Decision-Making and Age Review

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

## Automated decisions

No reviewed evidence currently shows a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect. Security rate limits, trial-abuse signals and delivery retries may affect access temporarily and therefore require documented human escalation and correction paths.

Required controls:

- inventory every scoring, blocking and profiling rule;
- document inputs, purpose, impact and false-positive handling;
- provide human review for consequential restrictions;
- prevent message content and recipient data from advertising profiling;
- expose an `AUTOMATED_DECISION_REVIEW` privacy request type;
- reassess when AI, ranking or fraud models are introduced.

## Children and target audience

LOGIVYA is designed as a business SaaS product, but a definitive minimum age, parental mechanism and market-specific wording have not been approved. Product and counsel must decide target audience and age requirements before public legal text or store declarations are finalized.

No age inference or biometric processing should be introduced without a new DPIA and explicit release review.
