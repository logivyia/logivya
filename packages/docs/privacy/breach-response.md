# Personal Data Breach Response

Status: `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

1. Detect and create a restricted `PrivacyBreach` record with discovery/occurrence time, affected system, categories, estimated subjects and risk.
2. Contain access, preserve privacy-safe evidence and rotate compromised credentials without logging secrets.
3. Determine controller/processor roles and notify affected customer controllers as required by approved contracts.
4. Counsel assesses jurisdictions, authority/data-subject notification duties and deadlines. Engineering does not assume a deadline.
5. Record containment, root cause, remediation and notification decisions/statuses.
6. Monitor remediation, link audit/incident evidence and close only with an outcome and legal review note.
7. Run a post-incident review and update DPIA, controls, provider register and retention where necessary.

All creation/status changes require a recently elevated platform administrator, reason and audit event. Breach records must not expose sensitive incident details to normal users.
