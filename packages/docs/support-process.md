# Support Process

1. Triage new tickets by impact and urgency.
2. Assign an admin and acknowledge the customer.
3. Keep internal notes separate from customer-visible replies.
4. Move through `OPEN`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `WAITING_FOR_ADMIN`, `WAITING_FOR_USER`, `RESOLVED`, and `CLOSED`.
5. Escalate security, billing, and data-loss issues immediately.

The authoritative API, authorization, notification, retention, migration, and incident procedures are documented in `docs/enterprise-support-center.md`.
